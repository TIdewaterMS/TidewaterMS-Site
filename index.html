About Our Company

Tidewater Marine Services LLC is a certified woman-owned small business specializing in commercial maritime support operations. We provide reliable, high-standard services designed to integrate seamlessly with prime contractors, federal agencies, and major marine construction firms.

Our operations focus on flexibility, safety, and rigorous compliance, offering partners a streamlined avenue to fulfill small business contracting requirements without compromising on maritime expertise.

Core Capabilities & Services

Dredging Support Operations

Expert coordination, logistics, and surface support for commercial dredging projects.

Crew Boat & Vessel Logistics

Safe and efficient transport, staging, and operational support for commercial-type marine vessels.

Prime Contractor Solutions

Providing primary and sub-tier partners with a dependable, woman-owned small business entity to meet contracting participation goals.

Marine Project Consulting

Operational oversight, regulatory guidance, and administrative management for marine and waterfront initiatives.
