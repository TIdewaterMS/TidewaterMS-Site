Privacy Policy

Tidewater Marine Services LLC

Effective October 29, 2026

This policy describes how Tidewater Marine Services LLC ("Tidewater," "we," "us") handles information through this website and through Gmail Connector, a private internal tool we use to manage our own email.

This website

This website is informational. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or sign-up forms, and we do not collect personal information through it. If you email us, we use your message and contact details only to respond to you.

Gmail Connector

Gmail Connector is an internal tool that lets Tidewater's own staff use an AI assistant to read, search, organize, and draft email in Gmail accounts that belong to Tidewater and its staff. It is not offered to the public, and only accounts we have specifically authorized can use it.

When an authorized account holder connects their Gmail account, the tool requests permission to read, compose, send, label, and move messages to Trash in that account. It cannot permanently delete email.

  • Where data is stored. Access tokens are stored only on the account holder's own computer. Tidewater does not operate a server that stores email content.
  • How data is used. Email data is used only to carry out tasks the account holder requests, such as finding a message, summarizing a thread, or drafting a reply.
  • Sharing. We do not sell, rent, or share Gmail data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • AI assistant. When the account holder asks the AI assistant to work with email, the relevant content is processed by that assistant's provider under its own terms, solely to complete the request.
  • Revoking access. An account holder can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Tidewater's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.
